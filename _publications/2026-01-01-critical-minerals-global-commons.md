This project studies why states adopt different positions on deep-sea mining and how distributive conflict shapes governance of critical minerals in the global commons.

Selected presentations include the UCSD PoliSci IR Workshop, UCSD PoliSci Methods Workshop, UCSD PoliSci Integrated Workshop, PIPC 2026, and APSA 2026.
